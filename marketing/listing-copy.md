## Long (~180 words) — for pages that allow full descriptions

humAIn is Australia's conference on what remains irreducibly human as AI takes
on more of the cognitive load in marketing, media and the creative industries.
The 2026 edition — the fourth, and the first as a fully independent event after
three years under the Mumbrella/Unmade banner — runs 13–14 October at Stone &
Chalk, Tech Central Innovation Hub in Sydney.

Day one is a conference capped at 250 delegates in a single room: main-stage
sessions and round tables for CMOs, marketing and agency leaders, and creative
executives. Day two is a hands-on workshop programme capped at 100. At the last
edition, 56% of delegates were marketing leaders and 81% were senior
decision-makers.

The programme takes on the questions this audience is actually facing: how brand
visibility changes when search and recommendation are mediated by AI, which
parts of the marketing operation to automate and which to keep deliberately
human, what AI does to the economics of agencies and creative production, and
how brands hold trust as synthetic content becomes ubiquitous.

Tickets are on Humanitix: early bird to 25 August 2026 — Conference $595,
Workshop $475, Conference + Workshop $795, all + GST.
