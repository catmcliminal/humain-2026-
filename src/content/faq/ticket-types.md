There are three early-bird ticket options, all prices are +GST:

- **Conference pass — $595** — day one: all main-stage sessions and round tables.
- **Workshop pass — $475** — day two: the hands-on workshop programme.
- **Conference + Workshop — $795** — both days: main stage plus the full workshop programme.

Early-bird pricing closes **25 August 2026**. Tickets are available through Humanitix.
