SXSW Sydney ran from 2023 to 2025 and was cancelled in January 2026 after the NSW Government withdrew funding. That left October open for the creative, technology and culture audience it served.

humAIn runs 13–14 October 2026 at Stone & Chalk Tech Central in Sydney and covers the same territory — technology, creativity, culture — at a deliberately smaller scale: 250 delegates, a curated speaker list, and a senior audience. At the last edition, 56% of delegates were marketing leaders and 81% were senior decision-makers.
