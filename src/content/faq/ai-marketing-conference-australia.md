question: Is there an AI marketing conference in Australia?
category: About
order: 4
---

Yes. humAIn is Australia's conference on AI, marketing and creativity, held 13–14 October 2026 at Stone & Chalk, Tech Central Innovation Hub, Sydney. Day one is a conference capped at 250 delegates; day two is a hands-on workshop programme capped at 100. It is built for CMOs, marketing and agency leaders, and creative industry executives. humAIn ran for three editions under the Mumbrella/Unmade banner before becoming fully independent in 2026.
