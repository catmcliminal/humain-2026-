There are three early-bird ticket options, all prices are +GST:

- **Conference pass — $595** — access to all main-stage sessions across both days.
- **Workshop pass — $475** — access to the hands-on workshop program.
- **Conference + Workshop — $795** — both days of main-stage sessions plus the full workshop program.

Early-bird pricing closes **25 August 2026**. Tickets are available through Humanitix.
