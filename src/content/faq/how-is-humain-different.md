---
question: How is humAIn different from other AI conferences?
category: About
order: 7
---

Three things: scale, seniority and focus. The conference day is capped at 250 delegates in a single room — everyone hears the same conversations and can find each other afterwards. The audience skews senior: at the last edition, 81% of delegates were senior decision-makers. And the programme is specific to marketing, media and the creative industries — what AI does to brands, agencies, creative production and the people who make them — rather than a general technology agenda.
